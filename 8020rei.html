HOW TO READ THIS

What we could measure, and what Google would not return.

Method and limits
The query set
39 queries: five positioning themes crossed with seven commercial modifiers, plus four comparison queries. Run against Google, United States, English.
Captured versus uncaptured AI answers
Google returned an AI Overview on 38 of the 39 queries, but served 36 of them asynchronously — the answer box fires, and its contents are not returned to any measurement tool. 2 answers came back with full text and sources; 1 query returned no AI answer at all. Citation rate in the score is therefore computed only over the 2 captured answers, and we say so rather than quietly inflating the denominator.
Why that limitation does not change the conclusion
The AI-mention corpus is an independent census rather than a sample of our query set, and it is unaffected by the asynchronous problem. It puts 8020REI at 2 Google AI Overview mentions and 2 ChatGPT mentions against 184 to 998 for its four scaled rivals. Both methods agree.
Cited, named, absent
“Cited” means 8020rei.com is a source in the AI answer. “Named, not cited” means the brand is mentioned but is not the source. “Absent” means the AI answered without it. “AI answer not captured” means Google served the box asynchronously.
AI search volume
DataForSEO's estimate of how often a prompt of that shape is asked of the engine. It is the reason a citation on “is sms marketing legal?” at volume 1 is worth less than it looks.

THREE FINDINGS THAT MATTER
01

Google's AI does not know 8020REI is a company

On the two answers where ChatGPT uses 8020rei.com as a source, the entities it extracts are the TCPA, the FCC and the European Union. Not one is a brand, company or software entity. For the same corpus, ChatGPT resolves PropStream to the named entity “PropStream,” and reads BatchLeads and PropertyRadar as a “real estate data platform” and an “AI real estate lead platform.” 8020REI is currently understood as a source about telemarketing law, not as a vendor in this category.

02

Every citation the brand holds is an educational asset on a peripheral query

Four AI answers cite 8020rei.com: a skip-trace calculator, a glossary entry, an SMS-compliance guide, and one guide that was retrieved and then dropped in favour of ftc.gov. Zero citations on commercial category queries. The single answer where 8020REI is the dominant source — “is sms marketing legal?” — has an AI search volume of 1. The highest-volume win, “skip rates” at 70, is an ambiguous query where four fifths of the answer is about energy grids, Instagram and waste disposal.

03

The comparison pages rank on page one and still are not cited

8020rei.com owns the organic first page on all four comparison queries we tested — the /compare/ hub, /8020rei-vs-propstream/, /8020rei-vs-batchleads/ and the DealMachine post all rank, several above the rivals they name. Over the same period DealMachine accumulated 998 Google AI Overview mentions to 8020REI's 2. The content exists and ranks; it has not earned the entity status that gets a brand quoted.
