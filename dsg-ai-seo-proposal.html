Content planning

Prompt research + Content Calendar

1 · Keyword and prompt research

  • Full keyword and prompt universe for distributor CX, employee engagement, wallet share, AI strategy and consulting
  • Map each term to funnel stage, owning page and the competitor holding it; prioritize by gap, intent and volume

2 · Author hires

  • Recruit authors who have worked in distribution sales, operations, HR or analytics, not generalist writers
  • They write the 4 buyer guides a month under their own bylines, at $250 an article

3 · Quarterly content calendar

  • Each quarter: the pieces to ship, with target keyword or prompt, format, author (AI or expert) and URL
  • Reviewed with DSG before the quarter starts; progress tracked in the live dashboard

4 · Content optimization

  • Regular refresh pass on the software, speaking and consulting pages and everything we publish
  • Expand pages just outside the top results or dropping from AI answers, then re-promote them
