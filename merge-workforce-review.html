Merge for Workforce: content review

Strong for builders, missing for enterprise.

Merge has strong historical growth, but almost all of it sits in the consumer and builder sector. This review covers how to target the enterprise prompts and buyers instead.

  1. A sample of the “workforce” prompts Merge is missing from in AI Overviews
  2. The “workforce” content gaps and topics to cover for an enterprise audience
  3. Industry pages that target enterprise use cases for workforce

Merge is recommended on the developer questions it has always owned. On the enterprise questions a workforce buyer actually asks, it does not appear at all.

We ran five prompts that describe the Workforce product in a buyer’s own words, three times each, and recorded every vendor Google’s AI Overviews recommended. Merge was named in none of the fifteen runs. The vendors that were named are the ones a CIO will shortlist instead.

Almost all of them are developer MCP and API topics: “mcp vs api”, “rag vs mcp”, API-key how-tos. The visibility is real, and it is pointed at builders rather than at the people who buy Workforce.

What the answers draw on

Merge needs a direct-answer page per industry and use case, plus placement in off-site roundups and off-domain channels such as Reddit. The vendors above are being named because that material already exists about them.

Where we would start

The five prompts in Section 1 are the shortlist questions an enterprise buyer asks before they ever type the product name. None of them has a page on merge.dev today. The fastest path is one direct-answer page per cluster, then the three industry landing pages, then the off-site placement that gets Merge named in the roundups the assistants already read.
