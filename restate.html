IDENTITY IN AI OVERVIEWS

How the brand describes itself, and how Google's AI describes it.

What the brand says about itself

Durable Execution

Restate calls itself the durable runtime for agents and backends: a low-latency runtime for resilient, stateful applications, built around durable handlers, virtual objects, durable queues and timers, with Replit and Capital One named as proof.restate.dev, 2026

Differentiators it leads with: Durable execution runtime, not a workflow scheduler, Low latency and stateful, aimed at request-path code, Agent runtime positioning for AI platforms, Open source with a managed cloud and bring-your-own-cloud option.

What Google's AI says

Durable execution vendor, listed next to Temporal, Inngest and DBOS

On durable execution queries the AI treats Restate as one of the three or four credible options and usually quotes Temporal first.
On the comparison queries it quotes Restate's own pages, and on two of the four it quotes Hacker News and Reddit ahead of them.
The core gap
Restate presents itself across five surfaces: durable execution, workflow orchestration, AI agent runtime, microservice orchestration and event-driven workflows. The AI recognises exactly one of them. Two of the misses are mistranslations rather than losses. Ask about workflow orchestration and the answer is business process software, Wrike, Kissflow, Pega and Moxo. Ask about AI agent runtimes and the answer is security and observability vendors led by Orca Security. No durable execution vendor appears in either. The two real losses are event-driven workflows and microservice orchestration, where direct rivals are cited and Restate is not.

90-DAY ROADMAP

Three sprints from first wins to compounding category presence.

Month 1  ·  Days 1 to 30
  • Restate is cited on all seven durable execution queries but leads none of them. Rebuild the category explainer so it answers the definitional question first and the product question second, which is the shape the AI quotes from.
  • Strengthen the four comparison pages. Restate leads its own alternatives and Temporal comparisons, but on Restate vs Inngest and Restate vs DBOS the AI leans on Hacker News, Reddit and a rival's docs before the brand's own page.
Month 2  ·  Days 31 to 60
  • Publish event-driven workflow content that names the failure modes, not the tooling category. Temporal is cited on five of those seven queries and Restate on none, so this is a contested theme rather than a closed one.
  • Earn mentions in the independent explainers and community threads the AI already trusts here: developer blogs, Hacker News, Reddit and the conference talk transcripts that keep surfacing.
Month 3  ·  Days 61 to 90
  • Decide explicitly whether to contest microservice orchestration. Orkes, Camunda, IBM and Kong hold those answers with orchestration-engine framing that Restate does not currently use.
  • Re-measure the same 39 queries. Track lead source share on durable execution and first citations on event-driven workflows as the two signals that matter.
