Proposal

AI SEO Audit Engagement

This proposal covers a five-part AI SEO audit engagement. The work reviews how your site is crawled and understood by search engines and LLMs, what to do with your existing content, and how your page templates support citations and conversions. It also includes a set of Claude content flows your team can keep using after the engagement ends, plus a measurement setup for AI visibility.

Deliverables

  • 01Technical AI SEO Audit
  • 02Content Audit
  • 03AI Flows
  • 04Design Audit
  • 05Analytics
01

Technical AI SEO Audit

We will review how search engines and AI crawlers access, render and interpret your site. The audit covers crawlability, indexation, site structure and the structured signals that help LLMs understand what your product does and who it serves. Each finding comes with a recommended fix and a priority level.

Schema opportunities
We will identify the templates and pages where structured data such as Organization, Product, Article and FAQ schema can be added or improved, and provide the markup for each.
LLM intro text for robots.txt
We will write a short, plain-language introduction to your company and products for your robots.txt file, so AI crawlers have accurate context about your business before they read the rest of the site.
02

Content Audit

We will review the existing content on your blog and resource sections against rankings, traffic, AI citations and topical overlap. The goal is a focused content library where each page has a clear purpose and a single target query. The output is a page-by-page action list your team can work through in order of impact.

Optimize, delete or merge
Every URL in scope will receive a recommendation to optimize, delete or merge, along with the reasoning, the target keyword and the redirect destination where one applies.
Author review and feedback
We will review your current authors and bylines for subject-matter credibility and give feedback on author bios, expertise signals and writing quality.
03

AI Flows

We will build Claude flows your team can use to create new content in a consistent format. Each flow includes the research steps, prompts, brand guidelines and output structure needed to produce a complete draft. Your team remains responsible for final review and posting, and internal linking is outside the scope of these flows.

Listicle flow
A flow for ranked and comparison articles such as “best X tools,” the content type that has produced the highest conversion rates in our client work.
Use Case / Landing Page flow
A flow for use case and solution pages that connect a specific problem or workflow to your product.
Glossary flow
A flow for short definitional pages that target top-of-funnel terms and give LLMs a clear source to cite.
04

Design Audit

We will review the templates you use today and create wireframes designed to improve citations, rankings and conversions from your content. The wireframes cover page structure, heading hierarchy, answer placement, calls to action and supporting elements such as comparison tables and FAQs. Your design team can build from them directly or use them as a brief.

Blog
A wireframe for long-form articles that places a direct answer near the top, supports scannable sections and includes contextual product calls to action.
Use Case
A wireframe that ties a specific problem to your product, with space for proof points, product visuals and a clear conversion path.
Listicle
A wireframe for ranked and comparison lists with consistent entry blocks, a summary table and a defined position for your own product.
Glossary
A wireframe for definition pages with a concise answer at the top, supporting detail below and links to related terms and product pages.
05

Analytics

We recommend setting up a PromptWatch account to measure how often your brand is mentioned and cited in AI answers. PromptWatch tracks visibility across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and other models, and shows which of your pages and which third-party sources are being cited.

Account setup
We can set up and configure the account for you, including your brand, your main competitors and your site.
Top 50 prompts
We will identify the 50 prompts most relevant to your buyers and add them to tracking, so you have a baseline before new content goes live.
